How classes are run

The basics that help a student learn well, kept simple and done every week.

  • Small batches

    Fewer students in each class, so questions get asked and answered.

  • Individual attention

    Teachers give each student personal attention, not just the batch as a whole.

  • A test every week

    Weekly tests show what is clear and what needs another look.

  • Experienced teachers

    Every subject is taught by teachers with classroom experience.

  • Study material from your own teachers

    Notes and practice material come from the teachers who take the class.

  • Mentoring by qualified seniors

    Class XI and XII students are mentored by students who have qualified NEET, JEE, NATA and CUET.

  • AC classrooms

    Comfortable, air-conditioned rooms for long study sessions.

  • Easy to reach

    In RG Mall, Sector 9, Rohini, near Rohini West metro station.
